# Where 5dive is going

**Close your laptop. Your AI team keeps working.**

Own it. Run it from your phone.

**Vision:** every small business has an AI team working for it, one it owns, not rents.

**Mission:** hiring an AI team is as easy as hiring one person.

## 1. A team you hire and talk to

Each agent has a name, a face, a voice and a job. Hire a ready-made team in one tap and talk to it from your phone.

## 2. Yours, built your way

Mix Claude, Codex and other agents in one team, on the plans you already pay for. Add skills, plugins and whole teams like building blocks. Your server or ours. MIT, no lock-in.

## 3. Work you can see done

Agents share tasks, use websites, check each other's work and show you the result: a lead list, a published post, a fixed website.
