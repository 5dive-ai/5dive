# Where 5dive is going

**Close your laptop. Your AI team keeps working.**

Own it. Run it from your phone.

**Vision:** every small business has an AI team working for it, one it owns, not rents.

**Mission:** your team does real work from day one, and every day after.

## 1. Employees, not tools

Each agent has a name, a face, a voice and a job, and it owns the result. Hire a ready-made team in one tap and shape it to your business: change any agent's job and skills, or build one from scratch. Talk to them from your phone.

## 2. Yours, built your way

Mix Claude, Codex and other agents in one team, on the plans you already pay for. A small MIT bash core. Agent CLIs, tools, skills, plugins and whole teams snap on like building blocks. Your server, or one we set up for you. No lock-in.

## 3. Work you can see done

Agents share tasks, use websites, check each other's work and show you the result: a lead list, a published post, a fixed website.
