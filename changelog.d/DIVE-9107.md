## Unreleased — feat(team): export a whole team as one .tar.gz and import it back (DIVE-9107)

`5dive team export <team> [-o file.tar.gz] [--with-memory]` writes one file holding every member's agent pack (skills included) plus roles, reporting lines and loops. `5dive team import <file.tar.gz>` brings the whole team back on another box without touching the public registry; a bundle with a `../` or link member is refused before anything is extracted. Memory stays out unless `--with-memory` is passed.
