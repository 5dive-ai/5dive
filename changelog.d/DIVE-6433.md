## Unreleased — fix(gate): a human gate's header and buttons follow the owner's language (DIVE-6433)

A human gate (the "needs you" message with tap buttons) was always in English, including on boxes whose owners write to their agents in Russian. The gate's own text now follows the owner's language: the header, the Recommended line, the Approve/Deny/Done/Provided buttons, the options heading, the secret and manual instructions, the hourly reminder and the `/inbox` re-send.

The language comes from the `lite-lang` file the telegram plugin already writes beside the channel's `access.json` from the sender's Telegram language. `ru` gives Russian; anything else, or no file, gives English, and English output is byte-identical to before. Button data, `task answer --value`s and the on-box commands stay English, so a tap on a Russian button answers the gate exactly as the English one does.

The ask and the options are the filing agent's own words and are not machine-translated. Instead, a seat whose owner writes in Russian is told so in its dispatch line ("write a gate's --ask and --options in Russian"), and `task need` warns when such a seat files an ask with no Russian in it. `tests/gate_owner_language_unit.sh` covers the Russian render, language-neutral button data, English fallback and the warning.
