## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): a Codex seat whose turn was cut off by a dropped connection gets its task back within one tick, with the cause, instead of a silent 20-minute reclaim (DIVE-9316)

Box report 1450: Codex seats stopped mid-task and needed a nudge. When a turn ended on "stream disconnected", the task stayed in progress, the heartbeat read the seat as busy, and the idle reaper took the task 20+ minutes later with no reason given.

The Codex bridge (telegram-codex 0.5.55) now re-runs such a turn up to twice and, when it gives up, records `lastTurnError` in its health file. The heartbeat reads that: a Codex seat that is idle with nothing queued, whose last teammate turn on this claim was a transient drop the bridge gave up on, has the task requeued now with the error as the reason, so it is dispatched again. Once per task; a second cut-off is left to the usual rules. An older bridge writes no such record, and nothing changes.

Also: `HEARTBEAT_STALL_MIN_MINUTES` works again. A hard-coded 20 further down the file silently overrode it; the default stays 20.
