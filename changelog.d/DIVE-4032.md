## Unreleased — fix(agent): credential and output health veto process liveness (DIVE-4032)

`agent list` and `agent info` now lead with an operational verdict that degrades
an active service when its credential is absent, expired, or failed at startup.
Claude startup persists that failure until a credential-bearing restart, the
auth hint names the real command, recent historical output no longer claims the
seat is transacting now, and `agent <name> tui` explains dispatcher-only Codex
seats instead of attaching to transport logs as though they were an agent TUI.
