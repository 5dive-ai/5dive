## Unreleased — feat(task): a one-off reminder parks until a minute or a local time, and a held yearly reminder fires late (DIVE-6807)

An agent asked for "remind me in 10 minutes" had no one-off way to file it: `task add
--park-wake` took only `+Nh` or `+Nd`. Measured on even-branch 2026-10-23: the agent fell back to
a once-a-year cron template and a hand `task rm` after it fired. The same day a yearly template
(`38 20 23 10 *`) had its only slot held by the pacing floor, and the next matching slot was a
year away, so the owner never got the reminder.

`task add --park=<why> --park-wake=<when>` now takes `+Nm`, `+Nh`, `+Nd`, or a wall-clock
`'YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM'` read in `--tz=<zone>` (default: the box's zone, the same rule `--recurring`
follows). The row is one parked row that the heartbeat unparks at its minute. A time in the past,
loose date text, `--tz` on a relative wake and `--park-wake` with `--recurring` are refused, and
the created line names the wake in UTC and in the typed zone. `task park --wake` takes `+Nm` too;
its date form stays UTC.

When the pacing floor holds a template's slot and no other slot of that template comes within a
day, the hold is stamped `owed <slot> UTC` (shown under `blocked_by` in `task ls --recurring`)
and the first pass the meter admits fires it late, once. A template with another slot within the
day (daily, hourly) keeps today's rule: skip, and the next slot asks the meter again.

`tests/task_add_park_wake_unit.sh` (15 arms) and the K7 arms of
`tests/heartbeat_materialize_recurring_unit.sh` cover both; 11 arms are red on the pre-fix tree.
