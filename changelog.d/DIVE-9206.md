## Unreleased — fix(agent): removing an employee also stops its schedules and open tasks (DIVE-9206)

`5dive agent rm` took the seat away but left its work on the board. Its schedules, a Proactive "Check in with you" among them, kept firing twice a day for a seat that no longer existed, and the client's Tasks showed a cycle and a queued task under a name no longer on the team. Removing an employee now cancels its schedules and unfinished tasks (result "agent removed"), clears its Proactive level and log, and the receipt counts what it cancelled.

A schedule left by an older remove no longer fires either: when its assignee is not a seat on the box, the slot is skipped, stamped "assignee not a seat" and logged with how to cancel or reassign it. If the agent list cannot be read, nothing is held.
