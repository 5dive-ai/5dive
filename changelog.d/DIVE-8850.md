## Unreleased — fix(agent): the 5dive helper's welcome always reaches the owner's app (DIVE-8850)

On a paid 5dive server, the "5dive" helper's one welcome message now always lands in the owner's app chat. In 0.138.0 the server asked the helper to write it, and on some servers the helper answered in its own terminal instead, so the owner saw nothing and it was never tried again. The server now posts the welcome itself, as the helper: «Hi, I'm 5dive, your helper on this server. I help you set up 5dive and choose your agents. What does your business do?» (or, when the owner already told 5dive about the business, one or two agents to hire). It still goes out once, and is retried only while the app does not take it.

Servers already on 0.138.0 whose owner never got the welcome get it once after this update. Servers where the helper did write to the owner are left alone.
