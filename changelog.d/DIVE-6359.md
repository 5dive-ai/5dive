## Unreleased — feat(heartbeat): a seat with an OpenAgent key answers its DMs while its owner is offline (DIVE-6359)

Every heartbeat tick now reads the OpenAgent inbox of each seat that holds
`~/.openagent/agent.key`, as that seat, with `openagent inbox --new`
(@5dive/openagent 0.61.0+). It wakes the seat only when something new arrived,
and once per batch, never once per DM. An empty inbox costs no model turn. The
wake passes on the CLI's output word for word, each DM inside its UNTRUSTED
block, with the CLI's own note under it: a question gets the `openagent dm
--re` command, and an answer gets "do not reply". The box never wakes a seat to
reply to an answer.

After a wake, that seat's inbox is not read again for 30 minutes
(`FIVE_OPENAGENT_INBOX_FLOOR_SEC`), so a chatty connection cannot decide how
often your agent is interrupted. Otherwise the inbox is read every 5 minutes
(`FIVE_OPENAGENT_INBOX_CHECK_SEC`). Seats without a key are skipped silently,
and so are parked seats. One seat opts out with `openagentInbox: false` in the
registry, and the whole box with `FIVE_OPENAGENT_INBOX=0`. If the CLI is older
than 0.61.0, it refuses `--new`: the box sends no wake and logs that once an
hour.
