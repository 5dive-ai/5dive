## Unreleased — feat(task): `--tell-me` / `task watch` wake the filer once when its row closes (DIVE-5852)

An agent that promised its owner "I'll tell you when it's live" had nothing that told
it the row it filed had landed, so the promise rested on a guessed timer. The new
`--tell-me` flag on filing (or `5dive task watch <id>` on a row already filed) records
the calling seat, and when that row reaches done or cancelled — whoever closes it,
through `task done`/`cancel`, a verifier's `task verify` PASS, a loop gate step, a
loop-run close or the recurring-stall auto-cancel — that seat is woken exactly once with
the ident, the status, the result and the ask to check it is actually live before
pinging its owner.

Opt-in only: a row without the flag produces zero wakes and zero owner messages. One-shot
by `told_at`, so a reopen and second close wake nobody; a failed wake releases the claim
and warns the closer; closing your own watched row prints a reminder instead of waking
your own pane. `tests/task_tell_me_unit.sh` covers each close path plus the negative
controls.
