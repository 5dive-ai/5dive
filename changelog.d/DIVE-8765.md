## Unreleased — fix(agent): proactive loop no longer messages about what needs the owner (DIVE-8765)

The proactive loop's prompt listed "something that needs them" among the things worth a first
message. Gates, approvals and asks already reach the owner whatever the proactive setting is, so
the loop duplicated them. It now keeps to a result they were waiting for, a problem it found, or
one useful idea. Existing proactive loops keep their stored prompt; new loops get the new one.
