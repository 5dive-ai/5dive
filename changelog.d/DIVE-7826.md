## Unreleased — fix(local-only): Teams on an offline box reads as allowed, not as a leak (DIVE-7826)

With `config local-only allow teams`, `5dive config local-only check` still listed
Microsoft's messages reaching the Teams endpoint through this box's Caddy as a LEAK, so an
IT admin auditing an offline box saw a false leak. Inbound traffic to Caddy's web port now
counts under "Microsoft Teams (your IT allowed it)" when a `teams-<agent>` route is published.
Caddy dialing out, another process on 443, or a box with no Teams route all stay LEAK, and
shutting Teams again makes the route a LEAK too.

The channels refusal named Teams even while it was allowed ("Telegram, Discord, Buzz and
Microsoft Teams is turned off"). It now lists only the channels that are actually off, and
multi-item refusals say "are".

`tests/local_only_unit.sh` adds CADDY and REFUSAL arms plus a mutant that drops the Caddy
exception and reds the first arm.
