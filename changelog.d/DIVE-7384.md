## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): the gate-reminder health check stops calling a working reminder job dead (DIVE-7384)

- On a box with a gate whose owner has no connected chat, the heartbeat told the ops seat "the gate-ping batch looks dead" about four times a day. The batch was fine: it had found that gate undeliverable and was waiting its 24 hours before trying again. The check now waits with it, and still raises the alarm if a day and a half goes by with no new try.
