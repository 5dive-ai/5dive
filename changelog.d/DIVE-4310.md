## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): every wake-failure exit names its step, rc and stderr; `task gates` resolves (DIVE-4310)

- **heartbeat: "wake failed" now says WHAT failed.** DIVE-4279 gave the tmux
  `send-keys` step a reason; every other exit of `_hb_wake` still returned a bare
  1, and the tick's verdict was the bare `[name] wake failed — will retry next
  tick`. Each exit — `systemctl start`, the tmux session probe, the `/clear`
  injection, the nudge injection — now logs its own step, its exit code and the
  **first line** of what the tool wrote to stderr, and publishes the same string
  so the tick's one-line verdict and `heartbeat wake-task` carry the cause too.
  `systemctl start`'s `2>/dev/null` is gone: unit-not-found, a failed job and a
  masked unit are different strings, and all three used to read alike.
- **The injector reports which rail refused**, so the wake can quote it:
  dispatcher inbox (with its rc), the pane-safe guard (unreadable pane vs.
  credential prompt — different reasons), a `send-keys` step, or an unverified
  submit. The non-claude Enter loop's exhaustion — five Enters with the seat
  still idle — was returning 1 with nothing written anywhere, and now names
  itself.
- **`5dive task gates` is an alias of `5dive task inbox`**, the verb that lists
  every unanswered human gate. It used to return `unknown task command`.
- **`agent list` stops calling a fast-cadence seat OVERDUE while it is working.**
  The stall window was 2x the cadence, which at a 1-minute cadence is 120
  *seconds* — any turn longer than two minutes read as a stall. Measured on the
  control plane 2026-09-11 11:44Z: quinn and main2 both flagged OVERDUE while
  mid-grade, in ticks that logged them as `active (mid-turn/conversation)`. The
  window is now the longer of 2x the cadence and 15 minutes. A floor, never a
  cap: every cadence at or above 8 minutes is judged exactly as before, and a
  fast seat genuinely silent past 15 minutes still flags.
