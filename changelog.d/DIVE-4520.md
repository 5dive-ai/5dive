## Unreleased — fix(task): the auto-mergeable hint names `task merge`, and `task done` refuses to re-deliver a graded merge (DIVE-4520)

The board printed, on every auto-mergeable graded row, an instruction whose verb **destroyed the
standing of the only seat that could have merged it**. Running the hint is what stalled the row.

On a loop row `5dive task done` is not a close: it takes the maker→verifier routing fork and
re-delivers. On a row that is already graded PASS that re-delivery carries no new work, but it still
stamps `handoff_delivered_at=now` — and `_TASKS_TFV_SQL`'s DIVE-4357 conjunct then reads a delivery
clock later than every verdict clock as *a delivery the grade did not grade*. `_task_merge_preflight`
refuses the seat `graded_by` names, which is the one seat the DIVE-3474 rail accepts. Exit 0, no
warning, and a state strictly worse than before the tap. Measured on DIVE-4491 / #963, 2026-09-14, by
a seat doing exactly what the board told it to do.

DIVE-4512 (#966) fixed **which seat** the board names. It did not touch **what the board tells that
seat to run**, so the correctly-named seat was handed the verb that revokes its own standing.

Two halves, and both are needed:

- **`src/task/loops.sh`** — the `merge_hold_reason` cell now reads ``auto-mergeable at the graded sha
  — run `5dive task merge <ident>` ``. That removes the trigger.
- **`src/task/status.sh`** — `task done` on a row that is graded-and-waiting-on-a-merge now **refuses**
  instead of re-delivering, naming the verb that works and the seat that can run it. That makes the
  verb safe no matter what points at it — an older board line, a quoted screenshot, a person. The
  rule was already written on the wiki before this row existed and it did not stop the tap that
  measured it; text-only enforcement is a proven repeat-defect class here (DIVE-4440).

**The guard's predicate is the shared one.** `_TASKS_TFV_SQL` already means "graded PASS, bound to a
pull request, no live reject", and it is exactly what the board renders as `graded->merge:<seat>`.
Re-typing those conjuncts would be a second answer to a question the store already answers, and the
two would drift the first time either moved — DIVE-4357 moved one of them once already.

**Scoped to the close path, not to `_task_route_to_verifier`.** That helper is also `task deliver`'s
funnel, and a maker re-pointing a binding at a new pull request is delivering something the grade
never saw. That is a real delivery and still goes through. The escape from the refusal is the
existing `--force-redeliver="<why>"` (DIVE-4144), which already means "this unchanged re-delivery is
correct"; a second flag for the same sentence would leave the audit unable to say which was meant.

Acceptance is behavioural, in `tests/task_merge_disposition_unit.sh`: C1c/C1d grade the hint's verb
(both halves — a cell offering *both* verbs fails), and C9a–C9p tap `task done` on the DIVE-4491
fixture and ask the **shipped** `_task_merge_standing_sql` whether the graded seat still holds
standing afterwards, with the ACKing seat and a live-reject row as in-population negative controls.
The fixture's verdict clock is pushed 30 minutes back on purpose: at `datetime('now')`'s one-second
resolution an unguarded re-delivery ties rather than overtakes, and the arms could not fail.

Mutants, driven by hand: restoring the `task done` string reds 2 arms; deleting the guard call reds
6; widening the guard past merge-pending reds 2; downgrading the refusal to a warning reds 2.
