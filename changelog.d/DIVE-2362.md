## Unreleased — fix(agent): do not confirm an unconfirmed send (DIVE-2362)

`agent send` and its scoped `_deliver` path now report `sent:false` or
`delivered:false`, plus a stable reason, when the payload was typed but the pane
still appears unsubmitted after every retry. The final human-readable line also
says the delivery is unconfirmed instead of overwriting the warning with a green
success summary.
