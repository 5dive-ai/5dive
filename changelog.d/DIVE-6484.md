## Unreleased — fix(team): a new team's first task is in the owner's language (DIVE-6484)

A hired team's first row, the kickoff where the lead asks the owner to agree its plan, was always titled in English and worded as an instruction to the agent ("Tell your owner your plan before the team starts any work"), even for an owner who writes in Russian. The title is now written to the owner and in their language: "Agree the team's plan with you before work starts", «Согласовать с вами план команды до начала работы», or 开始工作前与你确认团队计划.

`team import` and `up` take `--lang=ru|en|zh`. Without it the language comes from the template's `lang:` (top level or `team.lang`), then from the lead's paired chat, then English. The kickoff's body stays the lead's English instructions and now says which language to write to the owner in; the lead's "First start" persona block says it too. That block was also cut short at its first apostrophe before, so only its heading reached the persona; it now arrives whole.

Every lookup of the kickoff still keys on its body marker, never its title, so a re-import in another language finds the same row. `tests/team_kickoff_lang_unit.sh` covers it (13 arms, all red on the previous tree).
