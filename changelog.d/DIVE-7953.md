## Unreleased — fix(share): the leak scan no longer refuses an agent over everyday words (DIVE-7953)

A customer's "Share" was refused on lines every templated agent carries. Seven of ten hits
were the scanner, not the agent: `Agent-to-agent` (read as an `agent-<name>` account),
team members named by role (`head`, `outreach`, `verifier`) firing on every ordinary use of
the word, and our own public `my.5dive.ai` / `5dive.ai` flagged as private hosts.

- `agent-to-<word>` is English, not an account; the name exemptions now apply
  case-insensitively, matching the case-insensitive rule they exempt from.
- Team-role words (head, outreach, verifier, scout, publisher, analyst, …) join `main`/`dev`
  on the narrow actor rule: "ask outreach to review" still refuses, "outreach" as a noun does not.
- Exactly the public 5dive.ai names are exempt (`5dive.ai`, `my.`, `a2a.`, `blog.`,
  `install.`, `openagent.`, `docs.`, `www.`). Every other subdomain, and every `*.5dive.com`
  customer box, is still a private host.
- The refusal reads "not shared: vesper's instructions … (each listed line: …)" — no stray
  quote, no "above" for lines a dashboard renders below.

Not changed: `sudo` as a bare word still fires (no narrower shape was testable without
letting real posture facts through), and real task ids like `DIVE-7439` still refuse.
