## Unreleased — fix(share): the no-address refusal and the address setter carry a reason in --json (DIVE-7862)

`agent share --json` on a box with no public address now answers `error.reason: "no_public_address"`, so the 5dive app can ask for the address in the Share step instead of showing a `sudo` command. `config public-address --json` answers `reason: hosted | invalid_address | unreachable`, and a probe miss reads in plain words without the box's loopback address.
