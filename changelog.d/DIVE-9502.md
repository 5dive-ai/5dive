## Unreleased — fix(task): a browser ask on a gate says the owner approves by tapping the card, not by typing yes (DIVE-9502)

When an agent puts a browser payment ask on a gate, the line it reads used to say "the owner's yes approves it", which agents took to mean a yes typed in chat. The owner typed one, the gate stayed pending, and nobody told them to tap. The line now says the owner approves by pressing Approve (or Yes) on the card, that a typed yes in chat does not approve, and how to cite a chat answer on the gate (`task answer <row> yes --channel-msg= --channel-proof=`) so the owner is sent the buttons again. Wording only: what approves is unchanged.
