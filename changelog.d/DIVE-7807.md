## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): a seat whose AI account refuses its turns keeps its messages, and the stall clock starts at the last row movement (DIVE-7807)

**Messages to a refused seat now wait.** When a seat's AI account refused every turn (out of
credit, or a lapsed login), the seat still went idle a second after each message, and idle was
the only thing the heartbeat checked before handing it the next queued message. So the whole
queue drained into turns that ended in an API error, each one logged "delivered". The heartbeat
now reads the seat's own record of its last turns (the same reading `agent info` uses) and holds
the queue while it shows a refusal since the seat last started. It logs that once:
`[a2a-queue] <seat>: spool held, the seat's AI account refused its last turn (<class>)`. Fixing
the account restarts the seat, and the queue drains on the next tick.

**The fleet-stall alert no longer counts real work as idle time.** Its idle clock was reset only
when a tick happened to see no stranded work, or saw a seat mid-turn. A row started and closed
between two ticks moved neither, so the alert could say "idle 75m" across a window in which a
seat closed five rows. The clock now starts at the latest row movement on the board (a row
started or closed, including one closed without ever being started).

`tests/heartbeat_seat_state_misread_unit.sh` grades both, each with a negative control and a
mutation arm.
