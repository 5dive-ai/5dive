## Unreleased — fix: a lead hires when its owner asks for someone, and waits only when the hire needs a plan upgrade (DIVE-5974)

"I need someone to…" or "hire a codex copywriter" is now the go-ahead for an admin-tier lead: it picks
the agent, hires it, then tells its owner who it hired and that "fire <name>" undoes it. It asks first
only when the hire needs a plan upgrade. This reverses the earlier "wait for their yes" rule on the
box file's go-ahead line, in `5dive market` / `market show`, and in `hire-link --create`'s next step.
Standard-tier agents are unchanged: they send the card or the hire link for their owner to tap.
