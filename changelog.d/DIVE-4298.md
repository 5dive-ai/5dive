## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): a finished turn with background shells alive is IDLE, not mid-turn (DIVE-4298)

A seat whose turn had ended but still had a background shell alive (an orphaned headless browser, a long `sleep`) read as mid-turn to the heartbeat for as long as that shell lived, so it could not take a task and was reaped or deferred instead. The heartbeat now classifies a pane at the idle marker with a "done HH:MM" line as idle even when Claude Code's native status says busy, and reaps shells that outlive the turn.
