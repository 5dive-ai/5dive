## Unreleased — feat(task): a PASS on a clean pull request merges at the verifier's close, and a hold names the seat that can clear it (DIVE-4137)

lodar, Telegram 2026-09-09 04:22Z: *"10 PRs and 21 branches on the 5dive-ai/5dive — i think
something is wrong with our merging to main or we forgetting to merge"*. Nothing was forgotten.
main measured nine open pull requests, all inside the maker→verifier→merge pipeline, and four of
them (#799, #807, #809 and the frontend #220) graded **PASS** and rendered `graded->merge:<maker>`.
The merge was routed **back to the maker** — who wakes cold, or is at WIP cap — so the pull request
waited until main pressed four buttons by hand. Meanwhile the seat that had just proved the head
sha was green did nothing with that evidence.

Two changes, and they are deliberately in two different verbs.

**`task verify` now RECORDS who owes the merge.** A new disposition (`src/task/delivery.sh`) asks
three questions of a graded, bound row: is it mergeable and clean **at the sha the verifier says it
graded** (DIVE-2656's rule read forwards), are the required checks green there, and is the diff one
a person has to look at (CODEOWNERS, schema paths, a user-facing surface). The answer is stored in
the new `merge_owner` / `merge_hold_reason` columns and the board renders it. A hold now names
**`main`** — a seat that can look — and names the **maker** only for the one condition a maker
alone can clear: a conflicted branch needing a rebase.

**`task done` now MERGES it.** Immediately before the DIVE-1830 "not merged" refusal, a caller that
holds merge standing on a row whose disposition reads `merge` gets the merge run for them, through
`_merge_do` — the same DIVE-3474 primitive `task merge` uses, with the same root-side re-derivation
of standing over `_TASKS_TFV_SQL AND graded_by = <this seat>`. **No new authority**: the predicate
is not widened by one byte, and the merge sits *before* the gate so the gate's own probe re-reads
the merge state afterwards and DIVE-2656 still compares what LANDED against what was GRADED.

The polarity is the safety argument, and it is asserted rather than implied: **every unknown is a
hold.** An unreadable head, an unreadable file list, an unstated `graded-sha:`, a merge state the
function has not been taught, a probe that could not run, a rail that refused — all of them leave
`_state` untouched and fall through to exactly the refusal that fires today. There is no path on
which this makes a close accept something the gate would otherwise have rejected; the worst a bug
in here can do is reproduce the behaviour we already have.

`UNSTABLE` is held on purpose even though branch protection would let it through: it means a
non-required check is red at the graded head, and this rail cannot tell a flaky one from a real
one. Holding costs a look; merging costs the thing that cannot be undone.

`merge_owner` is a pure `ALTER` with **no backfill** — every reader `COALESCE`s a NULL back to the
pre-DIVE-4137 `maker_agent` render, so no already-graded row changes how it paints.

`tests/task_merge_disposition_unit.sh` (43 arms) drives every disposition branch from a fixture —
no network, no sudo, no pull request. Mutation-graded by hand against four mutants, each killed:
the graded-sha comparison deleted (2 arms), `UNSTABLE` accepted (1), the risk verdict ignored (1),
and every hold routed to the maker — the defect itself, stated as a mutant (4).
