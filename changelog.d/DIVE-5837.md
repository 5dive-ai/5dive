## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): a seat on Claude Code's weekly wall is parked to its reset, not re-woken (DIVE-5837)

Claude Code's weekly limit banner (`You've hit your weekly limit · resets Oct 9, 4pm (UTC)`)
matched neither usage-wall matcher. So a seat that hit it was woken about 40 times a day for
nothing, and its row was escalated with the false note that every wake "decided not to start".

- Both wall matchers now recognise the weekly banner and its dated reset.
- An idle seat whose screen shows a wall in force is parked until the printed reset, or for
  an hour when none is printed. A reset more than 8 days out is treated as no reset, so a
  stale or quoted date can never park a row for months. A reset already past still requeues.
- `5dive doctor`'s "not transacting" fix now names a command that exists:
  `5dive agent auth status --agent=<seat> --probe`.
