## Unreleased — feat(heartbeat): worker seats with no human chat start each task fresh by default (DIVE-8189)

A new heartbeat enrolment (`agent create`, `heartbeat on`, and `org set` changing who a seat
reports to) now picks `fresh` from the org chart: **on** for a seat that reports to another
agent and has no human chat (`channels=none`), and for a pinned reviewer; **off** otherwise
and on codex seats. It prints the choice and why, e.g. `fresh=on (reports to main, no human
channel)`. `--fresh` / `--no-fresh` always win, and an existing enrolment is never changed.
