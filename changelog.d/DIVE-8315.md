## Unreleased — feat(email): each agent can have its own email address (DIVE-8315)

People can now email an agent like a colleague. Give it an address in a mailbox your firm already runs (Google Workspace or any IMAP and SMTP server), or on your server's own mail server for a domain you point at it: `sudo 5dive agent email connect <agent> …`. The mail arrives as a chat and the agent answers in the same thread, from its own address.

A first mail to someone new waits for your Allow, and while the newest message is an email the agent will not run root commands, pay, delete or change settings until you say yes on your own channel. A voicemail sent by email is transcribed on your server. Needs the email plugin: `sudo 5dive plugin install email`.
