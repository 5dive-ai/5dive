## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): an approval waiting more than two days keeps its daily reminder, and the false "gate-ping batch looks dead" alarm stops (DIVE-8974)

- A tier-1 approval (or secret, manual, access gate) with a recommendation kept getting reminders only until it was two days old. The two-day cut-off assumed the recommendation is applied then, which stopped being true for these gate types. They now keep their daily reminder.
- A reminder that reaches nobody (no paired bot, no lead found) is now marked as tried and retried in 24h on every route, not left unmarked. An unmarked gate made the ops seat's pinger check report "the gate-ping batch looks dead" every few hours while the batch was fine.
