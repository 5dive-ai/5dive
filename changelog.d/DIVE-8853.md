## Unreleased — feat(team): one "Team freedom" level sets how much the whole team asks you (DIVE-8853)

`sudo 5dive team freedom ask|balanced|yolo` sets every switch that asks the owner, for every agent
on the team, in one step: the browser's four asks (sending, posting, paying, deleting), the email
gates (new senders, first mail to a new address), Proactive, and each Claude agent's autonomy.
On YOLO nothing asks: not paying, not passwords, not deleting. It runs a new per-agent mode,
`autonomy=full`; the old `autonomy=yolo` keeps its hard gates and is what Balanced sets. A secret
the box does not hold still comes from the owner by secure link, and paying still stops at each
agent's wallet cap. `5dive team freedom --json` answers `{level, label, set_level, set_at, set_by,
differs, levels}`: a switch changed by hand since makes the level `custom`, listing what differs.
New agents take the team's level.
