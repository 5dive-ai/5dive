## Unreleased — feat(team): a partner's team imports from its own private packs (DIVE-6088)

`5dive team import <slug> --template=<path>|-` takes the template from a file or
stdin instead of the public registry, and a seat may now carry `pack_url:`, a
signed link to its pack. 5dive-api uses this to bring up a partner's team from
the partner's private registry, which no client box can read. Every seat's link
is fetched before any agent is created, so one refused link (a signature that
does not verify, or an expired link) leaves nothing half-made. The links are not
kept in the saved team manifest. A team imported by slug is unchanged.
