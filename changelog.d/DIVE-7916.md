## Unreleased — fix(agent): `agent restart` on a stopped agent marks it running, so nightly updates reach it again (DIVE-7916)

`agent stop X` then `agent restart X` brought X's service back up but left the registry
saying `desiredState=stopped`. The update sweep honours that park (DIVE-4033), so every
nightly restart for X was held forever while `agent list` said ready; one agent ran an
old release through eight nights that way. The `agent restart` verb (plain and `--defer`)
now records `desiredState=running` after a successful restart, the same write
`agent start` makes. A failed restart records nothing.

The internal reuses of the restart primitive do not record intent: the supervisor's
restart rung must never un-park a seat an operator stopped, and `agent recover` is the
lead's narrow verb, not a replacement for `agent start`.
`tests/agent_restart_desired_state_unit.sh` covers both directions, with a negative
control against the pre-fix tree.
