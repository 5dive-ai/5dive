## Unreleased — fix(supervisor): the ID-verification tripwire matches "age" as a whole word (DIVE-7324)

The supervisor watches each seat's screen for an ID or age check, and on a hit it pages the owner with advice to move the account to another provider the same day. Its pattern matched `age` as a prefix, so an idle seat whose last line said "…to confirm agents' plugin copies…" paged the owner as if it were stuck on an ID check. `identity` and `age` must now be whole words. A real "confirm your age" still trips, including at the end of a wrapped line.
