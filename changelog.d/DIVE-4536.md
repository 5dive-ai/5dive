## Unreleased — fix(supervisor): a usage meter is not a refusal, and a tool-permission confirm is a stall (DIVE-4536)

The supervisor read claude's status-bar usage meter (`Opus 5 · 5h: 17%  7d: 100%`)
as a model-capacity refusal. That line is on every pane at all times, so once an
account's weekly window filled, every idle seat classified `quota-exhausted` and
the no-progress ladder was HELD behind a wall that was never measured — and the
hold's own oracle is the row that classification files, so it could not expire.
The weekly reading still counts; the two-window meter shape no longer does, and a
genuine weekly wall is still measured from the account-usage snapshot.

The supervisor also could not see claude's built-in tool-permission confirm
(`Do you want to proceed? / ❯ 1. Yes / 2. No / Esc to cancel`), which fires even
under bypass permissions and is not an `AskUserQuestion`, so no hook sees it
either. It is now a second cause under `blocked-on-prompt`, and the supervisor
DECLINES it with Escape — never Yes — after ten minutes with no progress, writes
what it did on the seat's in-progress task, and pages a person only if the
keypress fails or the seat keeps coming back to a confirm.

Measured: one seat stood on a single keystroke for ~10 hours on a live row,
producing 60 held ticks and zero nudges.
