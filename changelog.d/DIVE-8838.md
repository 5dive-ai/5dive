## Unreleased — feat(agent): Proactive earns trust — act rate, a level-up only the owner can tap, and day-one memory (DIVE-8838)

`5dive agent proactive <name> --json` now shows `acted_last_10: {acted, sent}`: of the agent's last 10 check-in messages, how many the owner acted on. Once at least 8 of the last 10 were acted on and the level is not Very high, the next message carries one line: "You acted on 8 of my last 10. Want me to message you a bit more (Very high)?". The CLI decides it inside `claim`, so the line rides a message already under the daily cap. One offer per 30 days: Not now, or no answer, means none for 30 days. The status shows the latest offer as `offer`.

Only the owner raises the level now. Raising it, and `offer yes`, are root-only (the app's tap, over the exec rail). The agent itself may only lower its level or record a Not now (`offer not-now`), so no loop text can raise it. Going down stays automatic and silent.

Day one: the first time the level goes above Normal for an agent that knows little of its owner (under 3 owner facts), its memory is seeded once from this box: the owner's name, the first job the owner gave it at hire, and owner facts the owner's other agents here already hold. Each is marked `seeded: proactive`, low confidence, with a `valid_to`, so consolidation can expire or replace it. Never copied: anything that looks like a secret, task content, customer or client facts, another owner's agents' facts, or a fact that was itself seeded. The status shows `seeded`.
