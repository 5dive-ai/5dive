## Unreleased — fix(task show): a closed ungraded row says not graded (reason) (DIVE-8700)

`5dive task show` on a finished row that was never graded still said "graded by <seat> in its own session" (or "graded by a command"), the wording meant for a row that is still open. A seat once nearly deployed on that line. A closed row with no grade on record now says what happened: "not graded (closed by task done, no delivery bound)", or "no grade recorded" when a delivery was bound, and its verify line says "closed without a grader" instead of "no grader yet". Graded rows and open rows read as before.
