## Unreleased — feat(agent): Microsoft Teams and email answer on Codex (ChatGPT) agents (DIVE-8464)

A Codex agent could not have the Teams or email channel: `agent config` refused
teams off Claude, a plugin channel ran on Claude only, and the Codex dispatcher
knew two channels (telegram, dashboard). The enterprise page now offers Teams and
email with either setup, so a firm on a ChatGPT account needs them too.

On a Codex agent, `5dive-agent-start` now hands the dispatcher each Teams or
plugin channel as an adapter (`CODEX_DISPATCHER_PLUGIN_ADAPTERS`, a map of
channel to entry path, read from the plugin's manifest `codex_adapter`), starts
the dispatcher for those channels alone, and wires the plugin's own guard
(`codex_hook`, e.g. the email plugin's "no root commands while the turn is an
email") as a Codex PreToolUse hook. A plugin that names a guard which is missing
is NOT started. The adapter runs from a copy in the agent's own home
(`~/.5dive/codex-adapters/<channel>`, refreshed each start), because the plugin
store is root-owned and the adapter installs its dependencies beside itself.
`agent config`/`agent create` and the channel installer accept
teams, and a plugin channel whose manifest lists codex with a `codex_adapter`,
on Codex agents.

`tests/dive8464_codex_plugin_adapters_unit.sh`: 27 arms (predicate and its
delivery copy, store lookup, adapter and guard paths, a path out of the plugin
refused, a missing guard drops the channel, the hook stanza is added once and
re-pointed in place).
