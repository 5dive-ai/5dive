## Unreleased — fix(task): a secret gate with no chat channel prints its one-time link (DIVE-7151)

On a box with no chat channel, `task need --type=secret` minted no link, because minting
lived only in the alert path and no alert went out. Its line also said "answer on the
dashboard" on a box with no dashboard. So an agent told its owner to use a dashboard that
was not there, then said the box could not make a link. The link itself worked.

Now an unnotified secret gate filed by an admin seat (or root) mints the link anyway and
prints it first in the result line, on a step line of its own, and as `secret_link_url` in
`--json`, as the thing to send the owner. When the mint fails, the "no secure link in this
alert" warning says why and what to do instead: a domain, or the terminal. A standard seat
still never holds a link. It gets the reason and the terminal line.

"Dashboard" is now said only on a box linked to the app. `secret link` is first in
`secret --help`.
