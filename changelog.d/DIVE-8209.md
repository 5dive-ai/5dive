## Unreleased — feat(task): `task ls --brief` lists every row without its body (DIVE-8209)

`5dive task ls --json --brief` drops each task's body and keeps everything
else, its result included. The app's Tasks tab reads it, so a box with a long
task history still fits what the box can send to the app (4 MB); one such box
had grown past it and its Tasks tab could not load. `--no-body` is unchanged
and still drops both body and result.
