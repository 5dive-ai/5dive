## Unreleased — fix(ci): the harness-verdict probe can no longer hang a full-sweep shard for an hour (DIVE-9322)

The probe ran each harness under `timeout` with no kill-after, so a harness that ignored SIGTERM kept the probe waiting until the job's 60-minute cap cancelled it, turning main red and blocking the release cut (twice: DIVE-8634, DIVE-9321). Both `timeout` calls now escalate to SIGKILL 10s after the cap, and the probe reports that harness as `timed-out` and moves on.
