## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): a weekly wall parks at any hour, and a recurring template no longer holds rung 2 (DIVE-5896)

The DIVE-5837 park only fired in the last 12 hours before a weekly reset. A banner that prints
only a clock (`resets 4pm (UTC)`) was read as the nearest 4pm, so at 01:11Z it meant yesterday's
4pm, already passed, and the seat was woken and requeued every 30 minutes until 04:00Z.

- The reclaim park now reads a clock-only reset as the first one after the claim, so it parks
  until the reset at whatever hour it runs. A reset that has passed since the claim still requeues.
- A dated reset (`resets Oct 9, 4pm`) now wins over the `continuing automatically at 4pm` line
  printed under it, which made it read a day early or already passed.
- When the park skips because the reset has passed, the heartbeat log says so.
- The escalation ladder's "seat is busy with other work" hold no longer counts the stalled row's
  own recurring template, whose daily skip stamp looked like seat work and held it forever.
