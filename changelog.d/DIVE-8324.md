## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): a seat held by the pacing floor no longer reads OVERDUE, and the hold names the right guard (DIVE-8324)

- **Pace-held seats no longer read OVERDUE in `agent list`.** When every todo is held, the tick now records that decision for the seat (`held (pace)`, `held (tier)` or `held (pace+tier)`), the same way it already does for a seat with no work. The seat shows its reason instead of `!`, so nobody restarts a seat that is being held on purpose.
- **The hold line names the guard that held.** It used to say "tier guard held all N runnable todo(s)" and advise `task assign` whatever did the holding, including the pacing floor, which `task assign` cannot lift. It now names only the guards that held, each with its own exits: `task escalate` / `config pace-week` / `pace-5h` for the pacing floor, `task assign` / `heartbeat wake-task` for the tier guard. A tier-only hold reads exactly as before.
- **`tier-held` counts tier holds only.** A pace hold was counted twice, in `pace-held` and again in `tier-held`.

Harness: `tests/heartbeat_tier_head_of_line_unit.sh` gains arms that drive the real tick with a hard pace band: all pace-held, all tier-held (the negative control), and one of each. 9 of the new arms are red on the pre-fix tick. `tests/heartbeat_hold_remedy_and_stranded_seat_unit.sh` B1 now runs the hold-line builder and checks each guard's exits.
