## Unreleased — fix(people): the empty list names Teams knocking; a waiting knock names its answer (DIVE-8111)

`5dive people ls` with nobody on the list used to say every agent keeps its "Teams access", while a new Teams connection was holding a stranger's first message. It now says a new Teams connection lets in only people you add, and anyone else waits until you answer. A waiting knock is listed with the command that answers it: `sudo 5dive people knock answer <number> allow` (or admin, or no).
