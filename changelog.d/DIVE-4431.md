## Unreleased — fix(gates): grade the ask a person actually receives, not the tier it declares (DIVE-4431)

A gate filed by the org root reaches the paired human — the chart resolves nobody
above the root — but the rule that keeps a human's ask short and plain keyed on the
tier the filer declared, so it never ran on those gates. One arrived on a phone as a
90-word ask rendered down to its opening status sentence, over two buttons labelled
`A` and `B`.

The readability rule now asks who will actually READ the gate rather than what tier
it claims, and the one-line render prefers the sentence that carries the question
instead of always taking the first one. The word budget is unchanged, and gates
routed to a lead or a verifier are graded exactly as before.

The third item in that incident — buttons labelled `A` and `B`, which name no
outcome — is not in this change. Refusing a bare option set aborts the fixture
idiom 37 test harnesses carry, so it is being decided on its own row alongside the
prior question of whether a lint refusal may abort a harness at all.
