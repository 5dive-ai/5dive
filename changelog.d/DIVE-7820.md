## Unreleased — fix(agent): a message to a busy seat says how to stop it (DIVE-7820)

**When `agent send` reaches a seat that is mid-turn, the reply now names `5dive agent halt <name>`.**
A queued message, and an `--urgent` one, is read only after the seat's current turn ends, so a
"hold, do not pay" sent while it is paying arrived too late. The reply now says so, and gives
the one command that ends the turn first. A seat that cannot halt others is told to ask a lead
seat instead. The JSON reply gains an additive `halt_hint` key on the direct path; nothing else
in its shape changes, and the old first sentence of the queued reason is kept as its prefix.

`tests/a2a_drain_and_wake_fresh_unit.sh` and `tests/a2a_urgent_interrupt_unit.sh` grade the
pointer at depth 0 and 3, on the urgent path, and on the scoped path.
