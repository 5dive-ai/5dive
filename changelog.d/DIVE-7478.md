## Unreleased — feat(telegram): a new agent greets its owner the moment Telegram is connected (DIVE-7478)

A new agent's bot answered nothing for about 40 seconds after Connect, because
every bridge that reads it runs inside the agent's session. The owner typed
/start and "hey" into silence.

- The box now sends the greeting itself, right at connect (`agent create` with
  telegram, or the first telegram attach), and keeps "typing" up until the agent
  is ready, for up to 90 seconds. The greeting says it is starting up.
- An owner who has not pressed Start yet is greeted on their first message, in
  their language. Nothing they send is lost: the agent still gets every message.
- One greeting per chat: the agent's own bridge (claude, codex, grok,
  antigravity, opencode, pi) does not greet again. openclaw and hermes answer
  at once already and are unchanged.
- `FIVE_TG_CONNECT_GREET=0` turns it off.
