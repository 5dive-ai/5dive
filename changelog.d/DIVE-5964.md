## Unreleased — fix(agent): `agent ask` returns an answer taller than the pane (DIVE-5964)

A Claude seat runs on tmux's alternate screen, which keeps no scrollback, and paints
a finished answer in one go. An answer longer than the pane put its opening reply
marker above the top row before any poll could see it, so `agent ask` waited out its
whole timeout while the seat had answered. A new hire's first job hit this on any long
answer ("roast my homepage"): the job reported failed and no task reached Tasks.

When the pane holds no whole reply fence, `ask` now reads the fence from the seat's own
session transcript (newest two files, last 4 MiB, read as the seat, fence-only). The id
is fresh per ask, so only this ask's reply can match; the echoed instruction never does.
The scoped path gets the same read inside `agent _capture`, limited to the caller's own
minted id. A short answer the pane shows whole returns exactly as before, and seats
without a Claude transcript are unchanged.

`tests/ask_transcript_reply_unit.sh` L1/L2 (long answer, direct and scoped), NC (the
same arm times out with the read reverted), S1/S2 (short answer, council shape) and F1
(no fabrication from the echo or another id).
