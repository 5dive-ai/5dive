## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): the stall alarm no longer pages the lead about a row only a person can move (DIVE-8965)

A todo row assigned to a person (someone in the team's people list, not an agent) was counted as "stranded", so the fleet-stall alarm paged the lead every ~25 minutes until that person acted. Those rows now count as parked on the human, next to human-held gates, and the alarm names both: "N parked on the human (G gate(s), T todo row(s) assigned to a person)". A row assigned to an agent, or to a name that is neither an agent nor a person, still alarms as before.
