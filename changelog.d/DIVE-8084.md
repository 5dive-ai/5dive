## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): a question the seat already filed as a task gate is not forwarded again (DIVE-8084)

When a seat with no channel of its own ends a turn on a question right after running `5dive task need`, the heartbeat no longer sends that question to its lead or a person. The gate owns the ask and pages for it itself, so a gate that is held or withdrawn now pages nobody, as promised. A question in a turn that filed no gate is forwarded as before.
