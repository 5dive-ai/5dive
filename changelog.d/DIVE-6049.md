## Unreleased — fix(doctor): grade CLI freshness against the version `5dive update` would install (DIVE-6049)

`5dive doctor` no longer reports `host/cli-freshness: STALE … Fix: sudo 5dive update`
on a box that is exactly on its fleet stable-route target. The published-version
probe used by doctor, `update --check`, the supervisor and the digest now resolves
the target through the installer's own ladder (local override, canary opt-in, fleet
stable route, last-known), so between a tag cut and the route promoting it a box on
the route target reads `ok` and its provenance is checked against the route
target's bundle instead of `CANNOT TELL`. When the route does not answer and the
target comes from the last-known fall-through, doctor says `UNCONFIRMED` rather
than green.
