## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): a codex seat walled by its usage cap resumes on its own when the cap lifts (DIVE-7626)

A codex seat whose turn was refused for its usage limit used to stay dead after the
limit reset, until someone forced a wake (leo, 2026-11-03: 2h24m). Three fixes:

- A dispatcher seat (codex on Telegram or the dashboard) now reads idle or busy from
  the dispatcher's own health record, not from its log pane, which read "working"
  forever. `heartbeat wake-task` and the tick no longer refuse an idle seat as
  "mid-turn", before or after `agent restart`.
- The reset time survives a fresh session: when the newest rollout holds only the
  refusal, the reader takes the last reading from an older rollout, or the time the
  refusal printed ("try again at 1:25 PM"). The park keys to that time instead of a
  rolling 6h cap.
- Once that reset passes, the tick un-parks the held row, resets its clock and wakes
  the seat onto it. It no longer waits for a supervisor reading that stopped saying
  quota-exhausted the moment the reset passed.
