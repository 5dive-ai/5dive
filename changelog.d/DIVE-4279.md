### Fixed

- **heartbeat: a failed wake now names the step and quotes tmux (DIVE-4279).** The
  three `send-keys` calls in `_hb_send_line` (composer clear, payload, Enter) were
  bare `|| return 1` with `2>/dev/null`, so an operator got `nudge send failed` and
  nothing to act on. Each step now logs its own reason line with tmux's stderr, and
  a failed keystroke is checked against the seat's newest transcript before the wake
  is counted as failed — if the line demonstrably landed (the transcript gains a
  user record after the send), the tick counts it as woken, so `woke N` matches
  reality instead of reporting a false negative.
