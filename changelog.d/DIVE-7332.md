## Unreleased — fix(init): Telegram setup pairs only a message sent after the prompt, and asks who it is (DIVE-7332)

`5dive init` paired whoever had a message waiting on the bot, even one sent before setup began, and put them on the agent's allowlist without asking. Bot usernames are public, so a stranger who had messaged the bot could end up talking to your agent. Now init only counts a message sent after it says "send /start", shows who sent it, and asks "Let them talk to <agent>?" before pairing. You can say no and keep waiting, or pair later. `5dive agent telegram-discover` gains `--since=<unix-ts>` for this, and reports `sentAt`.
