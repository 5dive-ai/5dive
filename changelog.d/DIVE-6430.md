## Unreleased — fix(account): a standard seat's /account lists every account and /usage shows the board (DIVE-6430)

On a standard-isolation seat the Telegram /account picker had nothing to switch to, and
`account usage` answered "must run as root", even though DIVE-5367 gave the seat a rail for
both. `account list` ran as the seat, which cannot read the other profiles' credential files,
so every account but its current one came back with no signed-in types and the picker dropped
it. `account usage` crossed the rail only after a step that already required root, so the
cross never ran.

`account list` now reads over the same self-scoped `_self_account` rail (a new `list` read,
no arguments) on a seat that holds the grant, and `account usage` crosses before that root
step. A seat without the grant keeps its local read and its root refusal unchanged.
`tests/self_account_rail_unit.sh` gets a non-root arm for both commands, with `ensure_state`
stubbed to require root as the real one does.
