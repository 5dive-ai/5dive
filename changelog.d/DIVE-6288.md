## Unreleased — fix(update): `update --check` says the install receipt in the past tense and flags a canary receipt a newer tag has passed (DIVE-6288)

Two boxes reported it: `5dive update --check` printed "this box installs v0.91.0 (… newest
released tag …)" 78 minutes after v0.91.1 was published, while `doctor` in the same minute said
`update` installs 0.91.1. The clause reads the receipt the installer wrote on its last successful
run (DIVE-4294) and dropped its timestamp, so a past answer read as a present one, and a seat that
trusted it skipped the update.

The clause now reads `last resolved v0.91.0 at 2026-10-15T01:00:28Z (from <rung>)`. On the canary
rung, whose meaning is "the newest tag", a receipt older than `latest` adds
`(stale: v0.91.1 published since)`. A fleet-pinned rung (override, route, last-known) is never
marked. `--check` still does not re-run the resolver. `--json` keeps every field, plus one new one:
`resolvedStale` (null with no receipt).

`tests/cli_probe_route_target_unit.sh` gains six checks: canary stale, a route-pinned receipt
below `latest`, a current canary receipt, no receipt, the JSON fields, and a mutation. Five fail
on the tree before this change.
