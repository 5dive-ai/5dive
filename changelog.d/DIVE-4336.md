## Unreleased — fix(push): the changelog-fragment lint now runs at the maker's desk (DIVE-4336)

- The pre-push rail gained a `fragment` stage: it runs `scripts/lint-changelog-fragments.sh`
  — the exact script the required `title` CI context runs — against the push's own range,
  so a `feat`/`fix` branch with no `changelog.d/` entry, or one whose fragment the release
  fold would skip, is refused on the box instead of in CI. Six PRs went red on that step on
  2026-09-11, each costing a verifier a full grade round to report one missing file.
- The title and fragment stages now both report in a single push. They are both
  sub-second and both grade the same required context, so refusing on one and discovering
  the other on the next push was two rounds for two facts that cost nothing to learn together.
- Fixed an arm of `tests/pre_push_rail_unit.sh` that asserted the audited override receipt at a
  hard-coded `.git/` path. Since DIVE-4288 the rail writes that receipt to
  `/var/log/5dive/push-override.log` whenever root runs the push — i.e. on every delegated
  `5dive push` — so the arm was red exactly on the root-side run that gates a real push, while
  the rail was behaving correctly. It now grades the property the receipt owes: the rail names
  a path, and the reason is at that path.
