## Unreleased — fix(task): `task verify --no-done` on a closed row appends its grade under the recorded result instead of replacing it (DIVE-6248)

A customer box reported it: on a `done` row, `task verify --cmd=true --result=<text> --no-done`
printed PASS and replaced the maker's result with the verifier's text. Nothing warned, and the
ledger keeps only a sha256 of a result, so the old text could not be restored. A failing verify on
a closed row did the same. `task done --result=` on that row was already refused (DIVE-2464), and
the auto-close branch of `task verify` already kept the old text (DIVE-2067). The grade-only branch
had neither.

Now, on a `done` or `cancelled` row that carries a result, the grade is appended under it after a
dated seam, and the verb says how many bytes it kept. The old text comes first, so a `graded-sha:`
line in the grade is still the last one the merge gate reads. Open rows are unchanged: DIVE-2483
already appends there.

`tests/task_verify_over_closed_unit.sh` gains eight checks for the reported repro, a FAIL on a done
row and a cancelled row. Five of them fail on the tree before this change, and the existing
auto-close checks still pass.
