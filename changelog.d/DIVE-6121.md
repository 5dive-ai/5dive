## Unreleased — feat(rotation): a seat that hits its usage limit moves to an account with room, and a standard seat can do it for itself (DIVE-6121)

`5dive agent rotation rotate <seat> --wall=<reset-epoch>` is the move for a seat
that has just hit its account's usage limit. It moves the seat only to an
account in its pool whose usage another live seat measures under the limit,
parks the account it left until the reset, and does not move the seat again
within 30 minutes (`ROTATION_WALL_CHAIN_SECS`), so a destination that turns out
to be full does not start a chain of moves. With no account that has room, the
seat stays where it is and waits for the reset.

A seat may run that one form on itself without root: a standard seat's sudo
grant only allows the self-scoped account command, and the CLI passes
`rotate <itself> --wall=<epoch>` through it, the seat taken from the caller.
Any other seat name or flag goes the root way as before. The telegram plugin's
stop-failure hook (0.5.95) uses it, so a chat seat with rotation on now moves
within seconds of its limit and resumes the conversation on the new account,
where it used to wait hours for the reset. Task-boundary rotation and balance
mode are unchanged.
