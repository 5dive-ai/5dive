## Unreleased — test(codex): one gate across the Codex channel seam, fresh seat to dispatcher (DIVE-3963)

A Codex seat with `telegram` or `dashboard` in its channels does not receive work in its tmux
pane — a dispatcher drains a file inbox instead. That inbox is a contract spanning two repos,
with one writer in `5dive-cli` and the reader plus a second writer in `5dive-plugins`, and
nothing forcing the two to agree. When they stopped agreeing (DIVE-4036) the seat was deaf for
~2.2 days while `agent list` said active. Both sides were tested — against themselves. Nothing
tested the HANDOFF, so a field renamed on either side stayed green on both.

- **`tests/codex_channel_e2e_unit.sh`** moves a payload the real CLI produced into the real
  dispatcher and asserts what comes out: the real create-path env writer records a fresh seat's
  type and channels; the real delivery resolver turns that into dispatcher delivery and an inbox
  path; the real send writes a message there; the real `ChannelDispatcher` accepts it, starts a
  turn tagged with the originating channel, replies to that channel ONLY, queues a second
  channel behind it, and recovers the queue after a restart that interrupted a turn.
- **The receipt arm is the load-bearing one.** Delivery returns success only after the message
  is unlinked — drained — so "the write succeeded" (the assertion that stayed green through the
  outage) is not what the gate reads. Undrained and unwritable are graded as separate outcomes,
  by their reason text, and asserted not to be successes.
- **Cross-repo path parity**: the directory the CLI posts into is asserted to be the directory
  the dispatcher watches, rather than left to two literals in two repos.
- **No silent skip.** Without the `5dive-plugins` tree the end-to-end arms FAIL and name what
  they wanted; they do not skip. CI clones that tree (public, no secret) in every job that runs
  the corpus. A gate whose end-to-end arms quietly vanish on the runner is the false green this
  row exists to remove.

37 arms, ~0.5s, core tier. 12 mutants applied, 12 killed — including "report delivered without
waiting for the drain", "post under `.claude` instead of `.codex`", "a missing declaration falls
back to the pane", "a different channel steals the running turn", "a restart never tells the
route whose turn it lost", "collapse unwritable into undrained", and the harness's own "skip
instead of fail when the other repo is missing".
