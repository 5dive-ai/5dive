## Unreleased — feat(agent): share links can bring what the agent learned (DIVE-7806)

`5dive agent share <name> --with-memory` adds the agent's knowledge notes to its
share link. It is off by default. Only notes about what it learned travel, never
its notes about people or anything marked private, and every note goes through
the same privacy check as the rest of the share: one line naming a path, host or
person refuses the whole share and names that line. The share page says whether
the agent comes with its memory. Packs from seats with thousands of notes no
longer fail with "Argument list too long".
