## Unreleased — feat(account): read every Claude account's usage every 10 minutes, busy or not (DIVE-6713)

`5dive account usage` used to show only what a working seat last saw, so an account with no busy seat had no current number. With `5dive config account-poll=on` the heartbeat now asks the provider for each Claude account's 5h/7d every 9 minutes, through a call that produces no output token and runs in no seat. `5dive account poll` does it by hand. The table gains BURN/H (5h points per hour over the last hour), SEATS (who is on the account) and the reading's age; a login the provider refuses shows `stale` with its reason and its last numbers, never 0%. Off by default; nothing switches accounts or pings anyone.
