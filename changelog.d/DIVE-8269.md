## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): a Claude seat's `/clear` that already ran is no longer marked stuck (DIVE-8269)

When the heartbeat started a task fresh on a Claude seat, it sometimes still saw
`/clear` in the input box after pressing Enter, even though the clear had already
run. It then marked the healthy seat as stuck and made the task wait about 20
minutes. Now, if the seat's chat history has just started a new session that opens
with `/clear`, the clear counts as sent and the task follows at once. Without that
proof, nothing changes: the leftover line is erased and the wake fails loudly.
