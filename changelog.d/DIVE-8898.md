## Unreleased — fix(helper): the server helper names the app's real buttons; built seats get the new lines (DIVE-8898)

Asked how to connect your own Claude or ChatGPT, the 5dive helper on your server told you to open an agent's settings and tap «Model», a button that does not do that. It now names the app's real buttons: «Add your AI subscription» on My team (or «Add AI subscription ›» in an agent's menu), and gives the same kind of how-to for connecting Telegram, adding a tool, hiring and cancelling.

A helper seat already built on a box gets the new lines once, on its next tick; notes the owner added under them are kept.
