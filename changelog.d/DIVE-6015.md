## Unreleased — fix(agent): a timed-out `agent ask` no longer blames a half-written reply that was only the question's echo, and the stuck-question sweep leaves a fenced ask reply to its caller (DIVE-6015)

When `agent ask` timed out, it said "the reply fence was OPENED but never completed (try a
smaller --poll-secs)" for every delivered question. The reason was that the question's own
echoed reply-format hint carries the opening marker. A seat that printed nothing got the same
words. The diagnosis now counts only an opener the seat wrote, even when the pane wraps the
echoed pair, so a silent seat gets "DELIVERED … never emitted the reply fence".

The stuck-question sweep forwarded a seat's fenced `agent ask` reply that ended in "?" to its
lead, or to a person, as "waiting for an answer", while the asking caller was still polling
for that same reply. A turn holding a reply fence is now terminal for the sweep.

New tests: `tests/ask_timeout_fence_diag_unit.sh`, which covers D1-D4, O1-O3, U1, W1 and a
negative control, and arms Y0-Y3 in `tests/stuck_question_forward_unit.sh`. The pre-fix tree
is red on D1 and Y1.
