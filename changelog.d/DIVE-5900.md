## Unreleased — feat(identity): the identity doc tells an agent where its OpenAgent card is (DIVE-5900)

Asked "show me your card", an agent guessed an A2A agent card because nothing told it
it already had one. The rendered identity doc (`CLAUDE.md`) now carries a short
"Your card" section: the card is the agent's OpenAgent persona at the path the
renderer installed, what to show (name, role, tier, voice, behavior, plus the
portrait via `5dive agent avatar get <you>`), and that "card" never means an A2A
card unless the owner says A2A. Marketplace, partner and made agents, which ship
their own `CLAUDE.md`, get the same section appended by `agent import` and by the
pack-sync re-render, so already-hired agents pick it up on the next nightly
pack-sync (one idle-deferred restart each). The openagent skill stays out of the
default skill set.
