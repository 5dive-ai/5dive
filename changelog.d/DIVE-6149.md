## Unreleased — fix(rotation): walled seats spread onto accounts with room, including an account nobody is on, and keep their turn (DIVE-6149)

Rotation only trusted a fresh statusline from a seat already on an account, so an
account everyone had left stayed invisible after its 5-hour window reset, while
walled seats piled onto one destination and walled it within minutes. Now:

- An account with no fresh reading is a destination when its last remembered
  reading proves room: its 5-hour window has reset since, and its weekly window
  read under the bar with its reset still ahead (or has reset too). A fresh
  reading at the wall always wins.
- Destinations are spread by the seats already bound to each, so several seats
  walling in the same window fan out instead of landing on one account.
- A seat that just moved no longer makes its new account look walled with the
  cache it rendered on the old one.
- The supervisor's wall move resumes the seat's session, answering a chat turn
  that hit the wall instead of restarting fresh.
