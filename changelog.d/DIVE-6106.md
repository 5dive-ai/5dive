## Unreleased — feat(rotation): balance mode drains a seat pool's Claude accounts evenly (DIVE-6106)

`5dive agent rotation set <seat> --balance=true` turns on balance mode for a
seat (off by default). At a task boundary, and only when the seat is idle, its
next wake is fresh and the task has not started, the seat moves when its
account's weekly usage is more than 15 points above the least-used account
whose 5-hour usage is under 60%. The destination is the lowest weekly account;
accounts within 5 points of each other go to the one with the fewest active
seats. At most one move per seat every 3 hours and one move fleet-wide per
heartbeat tick. Stale readings are refreshed from the live rate-limit headers,
and a reading cached before the seat was bound to its account is ignored. Each
move logs one heartbeat line with the seat, both accounts and their weekly
usage. Seats with non-fresh wakes are never balance-moved.
