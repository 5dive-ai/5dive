## Unreleased — feat(onboarder): firms get a chat link, and an agent only when the link opens

`sudo 5dive onboarder add <slug> --brief=<file>` gives a prospect firm its chat link and prints it. The brief may carry the contact's work email; it stays in the local firm table (`ONBOARDER_FIRMS_DB`, sqlite) and is cut before the brief is sent. `onboarder ls [--json]` shows each firm's opened, joined, messages, Buy clicks and purchases, `onboarder rm <slug>` takes the link down and keeps the row, and `onboarder mark <slug> --sent[=<when>] | --opted-out` records the outreach. A brief now also takes `field`, `size`, `country` and an `insight` (`{kind, text}`); emails and phone numbers are still refused.

On the onboarding server the verbs moved under `5dive onboarder box create|rm|ls|wake|tick`. `box create` stores the firm's brief and instructions only, with no agent. The first `box wake` adopts one of the warm, stopped seats (`ONBOARDER_WARM`, default 2), writes the firm's instructions and the chat so far into its root-owned CLAUDE.md and starts it. The minute `box tick` removes a seat quiet for 2 hours, starts one with a queued message and makes one new warm seat when the pool is short. `box ls --json` adds `facts` and `agent`.
