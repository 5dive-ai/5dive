## Unreleased — fix(rotation): probe a destination whose statusline shows 7d but no 5h before ruling it out (DIVE-6210)

Automatic account rotation could refuse to move a seat that was at its usage wall even when another
account had room. A statusline can carry a seven-day reading and no five-hour block. Every
destination reader needs a five-hour number, so that account read as unmeasured and was never a
destination. On 2026-10-14 this kept walled seats stuck until someone rotated them by hand.

When a candidate reads as unmeasured and there is no remembered proof of headroom for it, rotation now
makes one live rate-limit probe (`account_live_ratelimits`, which balance mode already uses). The
account is admitted only if both windows read under `HEADROOM_MAX_PCT`. The reading is stored in the
account's `usage.json` as `source=probe`, so later boundaries within `HEADROOM_FRESH_SECS` (600s by default) do not probe
again. A failed probe still means no destination; an unmeasured account never counts as 0%.

`tests/rotation_dark_account_unit.sh` arm 6 replays the 04:4x snapshot: walled seats land on the
7d-only account, it is probed once, a 100%-7d account and the account being left are never
destinations, and a down probe or a probe at the 5h wall moves nothing.
