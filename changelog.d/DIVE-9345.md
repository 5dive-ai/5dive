## Unreleased — fix(task): a gate card on another seat's bot is retired by root, not orphaned (DIVE-9345)

When a standard-tier seat closed a gate whose Telegram card had gone out through another seat's bot, it could not read that bot's token, marked the card "orphaned" and left it live in the owner's chat with working-looking buttons. Now the card stays live and the retire is handed to a new root-side step, `5dive _gate_card_retire` (an exact-path sudoers line on standard seats), which strikes or deletes it with the right bot. A card is marked orphaned only when the delivering seat is really gone.
