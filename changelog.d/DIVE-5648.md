## Unreleased — feat(task): an off-box gate owner keeps a remote team's approvals off the founder's phone (DIVE-5648)

`5dive task routing offbox <seat>` names the seat on ANOTHER box that manages this
box's org root. With it set, approval and decision gates below tier 2 do not ping
the paired human — not at filing, not on the re-nag, not on the 72h reminder. They
stay open in `task ls --gated` (route provenance `offbox:<seat>`) for that seat to
answer through its own command path. secret, manual and every tier-2 gate still
reach the human: an agent answer is refused on tier 2, so holding one would strand
it. `5dive task routing offbox off` turns it off, and open held gates re-nag on the
next sweep.
