## Unreleased — fix(agent): `agent list` shows how many messages wait in a seat's spool (DIVE-7307)

A message sent to a busy seat waits in that seat's spool until its next idle tick. `agent list` still read `idle (no work)` for that seat, so a lead took the order for lost and nearly sent it again or did the work itself.

The row now ends with `N spooled` (for example `scout ∿25d/30m idle (no work), 1 spooled`), `--json` carries `a2aSpooled` as a number, and a legend line says the message landed and should not be re-sent. The count is written by the spool's own writers and refreshed on every heartbeat tick, so a lead without sudo sees it too, and it clears once the spool drains. A seat with an empty spool renders exactly as before.
