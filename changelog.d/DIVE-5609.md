## Unreleased — fix(agent): removing an agent moves its reports to its own manager, so firing a team's middle manager keeps the people under it on the team (DIVE-5609)

`agent rm` deleted the org-chart row and let `ON DELETE SET NULL` cut each direct report loose as a
root. On a hired team (startup: `cmo` over `creative` and `researcher`) that took the reports off the
team: the Mini App and dashboard read a team as everyone under its lead in the org chart. The reports
now move to the removed agent's manager first. A removed root has no manager, so its reports become
roots, as before. `org rm` is unchanged.
