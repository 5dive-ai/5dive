## Unreleased — feat(local): generic work-account sign-in hook on the office server (DIVE-9264)

The office server can now let staff in with their firm's work account, once the Enterprise office app carries a sign-in module. Core stays provider-neutral: people on the Team list can carry a work email (set on invite, or by the owner), and a module inside the signed office app can say "this person is <work email>". A live person with that email gets a new device with their existing access; someone invited by that email and not yet joined uses their invite (its agents, within its 24 hours); with the office's say-so a new person joins with no agents; someone removed from the team is always refused. Without the module nothing changes, and invite codes and join links keep working everywhere.
