## Unreleased — ci(release): before 1.0, a breaking change cuts a minor

The first `!:` subject in the repo's history (#1430) made `release-cut` derive a major,
so the only cut that could pass was v1.0.0. Every auto and minor dispatch refused, and
the fleet stayed on v0.102.2. lodar chose to stay on 0.x (2026-10-26).

While the major is 0, a `!` subject or a `BREAKING CHANGE` trailer now cuts a minor, and
the release notes say so. 1.0 is still declared on purpose by dispatching `level=major`.
From 1.0 on, a breaking change cuts a major as before.

`tests/release_cut_assign_unit.sh` adds 5 arms and moves the old 0.x major arm to a 1.x tree.
