## Unreleased — fix(agent): a send to a busy seat no longer exits 1 after the message was queued (DIVE-6586)

`5dive agent send` to a seat that was mid-turn queues the message and should return
`sent:false, queued:true`. After the message was queued, the send read the queue depth
for its receipt. When that read failed, the send exited 1 and printed nothing, and the
log row had no `queued=1`. The message was still delivered later, so the sender read
rc 1 as "not sent" and sent it again (six copies on one box, 2026-10-19).

The depth read and the reason lookups after delivery are now non-fatal, in both
`agent send` and the scoped `_deliver` path. A failed depth read is logged as
`spool_depth=unknown`. The log row still carries `queued=1`, and the receipt is the
normal queued one.

`tests/agent_send_unconfirmed_unit.sh` arms T27–T30 drive both paths with a failing
depth read and errexit on. On the unfixed source they exit 1 with empty stdout and no
`queued=1`, which is what the box showed.
