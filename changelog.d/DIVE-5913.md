## Unreleased — feat(task): a recurring task fires in the box's time zone (DIVE-5913)

A box now runs on its owner's clock (DIVE-5909), but recurring tasks still read their schedule
in UTC. An agent that saw 08:00 in `date` and filed `--recurring="0 8 * * *"` got a task at
15:00 in Bangkok. Each recurring task now stores its time zone:

- **New ones take the box's zone** when they are filed, or `--tz=<zone>`. The created line,
  `task show` and `task ls --recurring` print the zone next to the schedule.
- **Existing ones stay UTC** and fire at the same times as before. `5dive task set-tz <task> <zone|box>`
  moves one to local time.
- **Daylight saving follows system cron.** A fixed-time task whose time is skipped when clocks go
  forward fires once, right after the change. When clocks go back it does not fire a second time.
