## Unreleased — fix(agent): an Antigravity agent whose Google account fails the age check says so (DIVE-7615)

A Google account that has not passed Google's age check signs in to Antigravity fine, and then
the agent answers every message with "Eligibility check failed … you must be 18 years old or
older". 5dive missed it: the agent read signed in and ready, and `agent ask` blamed "unsent text"
in the composer.

The agent's health now reads **refused**, with the reason in plain words: "This Google account
can't use Antigravity yet: Google needs to verify the account holder is 18+. Verify it at
myaccount.google.com, or sign in with another account." `agent ask` returns the same reason
(`refused: true`) instead of the unsent-text message, and a timed-out ask names it too.
