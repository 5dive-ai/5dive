## Unreleased — fix(goal): a big goal with a long description reaches its approval instead of failing (DIVE-8837)

A goal whose plan has more than 6 steps waits for a person's approval. The approval question quoted the whole goal, so any goal written in more than about 11 words broke the 25-word limit on that question: the approval was refused, the goal ended `failed` with "plan finish failed", and a "Goal: …" task was left behind with nothing to approve.

The question now names the goal in as many words as the limit leaves («Approve the 8-step plan for "Open a small online shop for our handmade…"? Plans over 6 steps need your OK. Full plan in the task.»), and says "your goal" when the text reads like an internal name. The full goal stays in the task's title and body. If the approval still cannot be filed, the goal leaves no task or project behind, and `goal status` reports the real reason instead of "plan finish failed".
