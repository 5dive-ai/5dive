## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): the log says what happened, not what it tried (DIVE-7684)

A failed escalation no longer logs "sent" or "flushed to '<seat>'" and no longer stamps its
throttle. The stall alarm and the daily ops digest now retry on the next tick, and digest
notices that were not delivered go back into the spool instead of being overwritten. The
rebalance line names the lead a message actually reached.

The fleet-stall probe counts only seats whose heartbeat is on. A box that keeps disabled seats
can say "🛑 fleet-stall" again, while an enabled seat it cannot read still makes it a question.

A parked seat no longer gets a "— waking" line every tick: that line is printed only after a
wake that worked, and a failed wake's line names its task.
