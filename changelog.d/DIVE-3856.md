## Unreleased — fix(supervisor): rung 4 verifies its own remedy; rotation stops reporting ok for a channel it cannot observe (DIVE-3856)

On 2026-08-31 the seat that gates prod ships went **deaf on telegram** after its account rotated and
stayed deaf twenty minutes. Two verbs reported success for outcomes neither had observed.

**The supervisor's rung 4 now verifies its own restart.** It restarted the poller-dead seat and
recorded `{"rung":"restart","result":"ok"}` — where `ok` was `cmd_restart`'s **exit code**. The
seat's lifecycle log shows that restart spawned no channel launcher at all; the failure surfaced ten
minutes later as `restart-rate-limited`, which names the limiter as the reason a human is needed when
the reason is that the remedy did not work. After a poller-dead restart the supervisor now **polls
for the poller** (once a second, bounded) and records `ok` only when it is back,
`restart-ran-poller-still-dead` when it is not — and **escalates on that same tick** rather than ten
minutes later. `restart-ran-poller-unverified` is a third outcome for a type with no argv-stable
poller; it never escalates. The rung-4 rate limiter is unchanged: a restart that works is visible in
~9s, so a second inside the window is the signature of a seat restart does not fix.

The verification probe deliberately does **not** reuse the classifier's `_SUP_POLLER_PAT`. Measured
on this host, that pattern matches the plugin **launcher** (`bun run --cwd …/telegram/<ver> … start`)
and never matched the **poller** (`bun start.ts`) — which is why it survived the `server.ts`→
`start.ts` rename. It is correct when both are gone and blind when the launcher is alive and the
poller is not. The new predicate names the poller and both of its argv shapes.

**`agent rotation rotate` stops over-claiming.** It cannot wait for the poller: its restart is a
`systemd-run --on-active=1` transient unit that fires ~1s *after* the CLI process exits, deliberately
(a synchronous restart SIGTERMs the caller's own sudo subprocess, and rotation is often invoked from
inside the rotating seat's own bot). So its envelope now carries `channelBounceScheduled` and
`channelVerified` — `null`, not `false`, for a seat with no chat channel — and the human line names
the poller check as **OWED** and not verified by that command.

`warm_channel_capability_restart()` is deliberately not reused: it verifies `systemctl is-active`,
the unit and not the poller — the exact state this outage lives in.
