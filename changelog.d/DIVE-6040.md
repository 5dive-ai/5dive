## Unreleased — fix(heartbeat): a grade whose session ended without a verdict goes back to its grader instead of holding the seat busy forever (DIVE-6040)

A verifier-held delivery with no verdict was exempt from every reclaim rule except "the
claiming process restarted". When a grading turn ended without a verdict (a background
watch that was meant to wake the seat died with the turn), the row stayed `in_progress`,
the busy-guard counted it, and the grader was never dispatched again. Measured on one grader:
749 ticks of `busy — 1 in_progress, skip` over 17 hours with every bound pull request green.

Now, once such a row is older than `HEARTBEAT_GRADE_ORPHAN_MIN` minutes (default 60) and the
seat reads at rest, the tick returns it to `todo` on the same verifier with the delivery
intact, and the same tick dispatches it back to that grader. A seat that is mid-turn keeps its
claim, a row with a recorded verdict is never re-graded (that stays DIVE-4724's hand-back),
and a quota-walled seat keeps its claim under the existing park.

New arms: [6040] G1-G4 in `tests/heartbeat_dispatcher_claim_unit.sh` (drives the real tick),
B6 in `tests/heartbeat_claim_outlives_verdict_unit.sh`, 2b in
`tests/heartbeat_reclaim_verifier_handoff_unit.sh`. The pre-fix tree is red on G1 and G1b.
