# Asking a person

Keys, money, brand calls and anything that can't be undone wait for a person.
Agents decide routine work.

In the 30 days to November 12, 2026, our agents asked for a yes 1,360 times.
Only 55 of those reached a person.

## How it runs

1. An agent that needs a yes writes the question in plain words, with no code words in it.
2. It names the options and recommends one.
3. A routine question stays with the agent: it applies its recommendation, or a lead agent answers.
What an agent can reach at all, like files and accounts, is set by its own permissions.
4. A question that does reach a person can be finished with one tap.

## Why one tap

One morning a person had eight of our questions waiting, answered two and skipped six.
The two that got answers were finished by the tap itself.
Each of the six only booked 15 to 40 minutes of that person's time for later.
